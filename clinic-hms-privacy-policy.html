Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using Our Service, We ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify You, including:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Date of birth
  • Gender
  • An emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship to You (optional, provided by You)

Health Data

Because HealthNet is a healthcare appointment and records application, We also collect and store health information necessary to provide the Service, including:

  • Blood group and known allergies
  • Diagnoses, prescriptions, and doctor's notes from visits booked through the Service
  • Vaccination records
  • Lab and diagnostic reports and results
  • Appointment and visit history with doctors and hospitals on the platform

This health information is only ever visible to You and the doctors and hospital staff directly involved in Your care.

Family Member (Dependent) Data

If You add a family member, such as a child or parent, to Your account to manage their appointments and records, We collect the same categories of personal and health data listed above for that dependent. This information is entered and managed by You, the account holder — a dependent added this way does not create or access their own separate account.

Photos and Documents

With Your permission, the Service lets You take or upload a photo for Your profile picture, and photograph (using Your Device's camera, only when You choose to take a photo) or upload one or many medical documents at once — lab reports, prescriptions, bills, scans/imaging, discharge summaries, and vaccination certificates — to attach to Your records. When You upload, You choose whether the document belongs to You or to a family member on Your account. The uploaded file is stored securely on Our cloud storage and stays in Your records until You delete it or Your account. Only files that could not be read (failed uploads) can be removed from within the app; to remove any other document, contact Us as described under "Delete Your Account and Personal Data".

Sorting and Reviewing Uploaded Documents

To help sort the documents You upload (for example into Prescription, Lab Report, Medical Bill, Imaging Report or Discharge Summary) and to read the printed date on them, the text in each document is read on Our own servers using text recognition (OCR), and then matched against a fixed set of keyword rules. We do not send Your documents or their text to any third-party artificial intelligence (AI) service. The result is only a suggestion: after a document is read, You review the suggested type and confirm it or change it before it is added to Your records. A document that has not yet been confirmed by You is kept private to You and is not shown to doctors through Share Records. This sorting is an aid only and is not medical advice or a diagnosis. Your documents are not used to advertise to You and are not sold.

Notifications

If You allow notifications, the app shows a notification on Your Device when documents You uploaded have finished being read and are ready for You to review, and may register a notification token for Your Device with Our server for this purpose. Allowing notifications is optional, and You can turn them off at any time in Your Device settings. The notification token is used only to notify You about Your own account activity and is not used for advertising.

Sharing Your Records With a Doctor (Share Records)

You can let a doctor view Your records for a short time. You create a one-time code in the app; the doctor enters it on Our website, and You approve the request by scanning the doctor's screen or confirming in the app. Access lasts for 2 hours and You can end it at any time. You decide what a doctor can see: in "Shared records privacy" You can hide any individual document or whole category from a shared view. We keep a record of share requests and approvals for Your security.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect Your Device's precise location, and We do not access Your phone's contacts/address book. We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or any other third-party analytics or advertising SDK. We do not use third-party artificial intelligence services to process Your documents.

Use of Your Personal Data

The Company may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain Our Service, including Your account, appointment booking, medical records, prescriptions, and reminders.
  • To deliver Your care, by sharing relevant health information with the specific doctors and hospital staff You book an appointment with.
  • To contact You, by sending one-time passwords (OTP) for login, registration, and password reset, and appointment confirmations and reminders. We do not send marketing communications.
  • To manage Your requests: to attend and manage requests You submit to Us.
  • For business transfers: We may use Your Personal Data to evaluate or conduct a merger, divestiture, restructuring, reorganization, dissolution, or other sale or transfer of some or all of Our assets.

We may share Your Personal Data in the following situations:

  • With the hospitals and doctors You book with, so they can provide medical care.
  • With Service Providers who host or operate the Service's infrastructure, such as cloud hosting and storage, and email and SMS delivery (for example, Your phone number is passed to an SMS provider to send You a one-time password).
  • With a doctor, only for the limited time and only the records You choose, when You use Share Records.
  • For business transfers, as described above, or with Your consent.

We do not sell Your Personal Data, and We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Retention of Your Personal Data

The Company will retain Your Personal Data, including medical records, only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will retain and use Your Personal Data for as long as Your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with applicable healthcare record-keeping and legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce Our legal agreements and policies.

Transfer of Your Personal Data

Your information, including Personal Data, is processed at the Company's operating offices and in any other places where the parties involved in the processing are located. This means that this information may be transferred to, and maintained on, computers located outside of Your state, province, country, or other governmental jurisdiction.

The Company will take all steps reasonably necessary to ensure that Your data is treated securely and in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Delete Your Account and Personal Data

You have the right to delete or request that We assist in deleting the Personal Data that We have collected about You. You may update or correct most of Your information at any time by signing in to Your Account and visiting the Personal Details section within the app.

Delete directly in the app (recommended)

Sign in to the app, open the Profile tab, tap Support, then tap Delete Account. You will be shown what is deleted, and asked to enter Your password and type DELETE to confirm. Once confirmed, You are signed out immediately and the deletion is completed within 2 to 5 working days.

If You cannot sign in

Send an email to rnd@atomwalk.com with the subject "Delete my HealthNet account" from the email address registered on Your account, and include the phone number registered on Your account so that We can verify it is You. We will confirm receipt and complete the deletion within 2 to 5 working days of verifying Your request (and no later than 30 days).

What is deleted

  • Your account and login details
  • Your profile information (name, phone number, email address, date of birth, gender, emergency contact)
  • Health information You added, including blood group, allergies, vaccination records, and uploaded reports, prescriptions and other documents
  • Information about family members (dependents) added to Your account
  • Any records-sharing codes and permissions You created
  • Notification tokens registered for Your Devices

What may be kept

Records of visits and treatment held by a hospital or doctor for care You received from them may be retained by that hospital as required by applicable healthcare record-keeping and legal obligations. We may also keep limited information where We have a legal obligation or lawful basis to do so, and only for as long as that obligation lasts. Once deleted, Your data cannot be recovered.
